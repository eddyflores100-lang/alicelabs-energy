Commercial energy procurement in Massachusetts

Massachusetts is one of 15 US states (plus Canada) with deregulated commercial energy markets, meaning businesses can choose their electricity and natural gas supplier instead of being locked into the utility default rate. Since deregulation in 1998, businesses in Massachusetts have access to the ISO-NE wholesale market.

AliceLabs, as an authorized affiliate of Bid On Energy, connects Massachusetts businesses to a competitive auction platform where 12+ certified energy suppliers — including Constellation, Direct Energy, Vistra, NRG, ENGIE, and Calpine — bid for your commercial electricity and natural gas contract. The result: typical savings of 25-40% below the utility default supply rate.
