Commercial energy procurement in Canada

Canada is one of 15 US states (plus Canada) with deregulated commercial energy markets, meaning businesses can choose their electricity and natural gas supplier instead of being locked into the utility default rate. Since deregulation in 2002, businesses in Canada have access to the IESO, AESO, BC Hydro wholesale markets.

AliceLabs, as an authorized affiliate of Bid On Energy, connects Canada businesses to a competitive auction platform where 12+ certified energy suppliers — including Constellation, Direct Energy, Vistra, NRG, ENGIE, and Calpine — bid for your commercial electricity and natural gas contract. The result: typical savings of 25-40% below the utility default supply rate.
