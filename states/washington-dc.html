Commercial energy procurement in Washington DC

Washington DC is one of 15 US states (plus Canada) with deregulated commercial energy markets, meaning businesses can choose their electricity supplier instead of being locked into the utility default rate. Since deregulation in 2001, businesses in Washington DC have access to the PJM wholesale market.

AliceLabs, as an authorized affiliate of Bid On Energy, connects Washington DC businesses to a competitive auction platform where 12+ certified energy suppliers — including Constellation, Direct Energy, Vistra, NRG, ENGIE, and Calpine — bid for your commercial electricity contract. The result: typical savings of 25-40% below the utility default supply rate.
