Commercial energy procurement in Rhode Island

Rhode Island is one of 15 US states (plus Canada) with deregulated commercial energy markets, meaning businesses can choose their electricity supplier instead of being locked into the utility default rate. Since deregulation in 1996, businesses in Rhode Island have access to the ISO-NE wholesale market.

AliceLabs, as an authorized affiliate of Bid On Energy, connects Rhode Island businesses to a competitive auction platform where 12+ certified energy suppliers — including Constellation, Direct Energy, Vistra, NRG, ENGIE, and Calpine — bid for your commercial electricity contract. The result: typical savings of 25-40% below the utility default supply rate.
